About me

From financing decisions to engineering better systems.

My path into software started on the business side. Six years in automotive finance taught me how to evaluate risk, earn trust, explain complicated decisions, and keep work moving when the stakes were real.

That experience also showed me how much better the work could be when the software truly fit the people and process. I followed that curiosity into computer science and found the work I wanted to keep doing: designing systems, solving technical problems, and turning an idea into something useful.

I earned my B.S. in Computer Science from Southern New Hampshire University in 2025, with an emphasis on software engineering and secure coding. Today I bring both sides of that career to full-stack development—business context from finance and the engineering depth to build the solution.
